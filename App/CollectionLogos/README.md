# Festival marks

Bundled PNGs in `../CollectionLogos.xcassets` use transparent backgrounds and white artwork.
They identify the festivals; all trademarks remain with their respective owners.
The app is a private, noncommercial client. No open licence is asserted for the
festival-supplied marks unless noted below.

- **Cannes** — [Palme d’Or silhouette](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Palme_d%27Or_gold_silhouette.svg), Festival de Cannes; Commons identifies the graphic as public domain.
- **Berlinale** — bear and signature from the inline SVG on the [official homepage](https://www.berlinale.de/en/home.html). Edition lettering omitted.
- **Venice** — festival lion isolated from the [2012 festival lockup](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Internationale_Filmfestspiele_von_Venedig_2012_Logo.svg); edition numerals and lettering omitted. Commons identifies the graphic as public domain, author unknown. This is the film festival mark, not the city seal.
- **Sundance** — wordmark from the [official festival SVG](https://d2wsrejhnxatgp.cloudfront.net/assest/prod1/sundance_logo.svg), linked by the [festival website](https://festival.sundance.org/). Background rectangle and edition year omitted.
- **Toronto** — [TIFF logo](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Toronto_International_Film_Festival_logo.svg), Toronto International Film Festival; Commons identifies the typographic logo as public domain.
- **Locarno** — [supplied logo PNG](https://www.fiafnet.org/images/tinyUpload/2020/12/LFF-GEN-2linee-small-POS.png) on the [FIAF supporter profile](https://www.fiafnet.org/pages/Community/Supporter-LOCARNO.html), Locarno Film Festival.
- **San Sebastián** — [official horizontal logo SVG](https://www.sansebastianfestival.com/admin_img/pag/logo_ssiff_horizontal_negro.svg), San Sebastián International Film Festival.

Original proportions and transparency are retained, with transparent margins trimmed.
Colour is normalised to white for the charcoal canvas. Source links are also available
on each collection page. Director photographs are remote Commons images; their
photographer, licence and source links are kept in `FilmCollections.swift`.
