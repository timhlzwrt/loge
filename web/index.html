What sets it apart

01 · Sources

Every stream, read for you

Resolution, HDR, codec, audio, size and bitrate from all your addons at once, grouped by quality. When your connection can’t carry the biggest file, loge marks the best one it can.

02 · Series

The next episode just plays

loge remembers the release you picked and plays the next episode from the same one. It skips intros, recaps and credits, counts down Up Next and falls back to another source when a link dies.

03 · Playback

mpv, with Picture in Picture

mpv draws through Apple’s own video layer, so MKV, HEVC and HLS play hardware-decoded and still float in Picture in Picture, show on the Lock Screen and go to AirPlay.

04 · Apple TV

A real Apple TV app

A Home that fills the screen with artwork as you move, the Siri Remote throughout, and the display switched to each film’s frame rate and dynamic range.

05 · Discovery

Something to watch before any addon

Popular titles, Letterboxd lists, festival winners and great directors are built in. With a free TMDB key, recommendations are ranked on your device from what you watch.

06 · Sync

Your progress, your server

Continue Watching follows you from phone to TV through Trakt, or through a sync service you host yourself, encrypted on the device.

Install

loge is sideloaded; a free Apple ID is enough. Every change to the app is a new build on GitHub Releases.

iPhone, iPad

.ipa

Add the SideStore source to get every build as an update, or sign the .ipa with iloader.

https://tjh.li/loge/source.json

Apple TV

.ipa

Install the tvOS build with atvloadly or Sideloadly.

Mac

.dmgApple silicon

Then open Settings → Addons and paste your provider’s manifest.json URL. Full guide ↗

Questions

Does loge come with anything to watch?

Catalogs to browse, not streams. Playback comes from the Stremio addons you install; loge does not host, bundle or sell any content.

Which addons work?

Any addon that returns HTTP(S) streams. Torrent results are left out, so torrent addons need a debrid service that turns them into links. Subtitle addons work too.

Why isn’t it in the App Store?

It is a personal project without a paid developer account. With a free Apple ID, iPhone and Apple TV installs last seven days; SideStore and atvloadly refresh them.

Can I download for offline viewing?

No, loge streams.

Something doesn’t work.

Open an issue with your device, the build number from Settings → About and what happened.
