Browse

Artwork first, from the first launch.

Popular films and series, Letterboxd lists, genres, festival winners and great directors are built in, so Home is full before you add anything. Catalogs from your own addons join them. Title pages bring seasons, Up Next, cast and crew, reviews and, with a free TMDB key, More Like This and a Recommended row ranked on your device.

Sources

Every stream, read for you.

All your addons are asked at once. loge reads each stream into resolution, HDR, codec, audio and size, groups them by quality and estimates the bitrate, so a 58 GB remux and a 560 MB encode are easy to tell apart.

  • Remembers the release you chose and plays the next episode from it.
  • Falls back to another source when a link fails.
  • Marks the best source your connection can carry.

Playback

mpv, with the controls you expect.

Hardware-decoded mpv plays MKV, HEVC, HLS and almost anything else an addon serves, behind controls that feel at home on each device.

Install

loge is installed by sideloading, like other open-source players. A free Apple ID is enough. Every change to the app becomes a new build on GitHub Releases.

  1. Download

    Get loge-iOS.ipa, loge-tvOS.ipa or loge-macOS.dmg from the latest release.

  2. Install

    iPhone, iPad
    Add the SideStore source to get every new build as an update, or sign the .ipa with iloader. https://tjh.li/loge/source.json
    Apple TV
    Install the tvOS .ipa with atvloadly or Sideloadly.
    Mac
    Drag loge into Applications. On first launch, choose Open Anyway in Privacy & Security. Updates then arrive in the app.

  3. Add an addon

    Open Settings → Addons and paste your provider’s manifest.json URL. Then open any title and press Play.

Questions

Does loge come with anything to watch?

It comes with catalogs to browse, not streams. Playback comes from the Stremio addons you install; loge does not host, bundle or sell content.

Which addons work?

Any Stremio addon that returns HTTP(S) streams. Torrent and magnet results are left out, so torrent-based addons need a debrid service that turns them into links. Subtitle addons such as OpenSubtitles work too.

Why isn’t it in the App Store?

It is a personal project without a paid developer account. With a free Apple ID, iPhone and Apple TV installs are signed for seven days; SideStore and atvloadly refresh them for you.

Can I download for offline viewing?

No. loge streams; keeping files offline is out of scope.

Which languages does it support?

The interface is in English. Audio and subtitle languages are chosen separately in Settings, and loge picks matching tracks automatically.

Something doesn’t work.

loge is young and changes often. Please open an issue with your device, the build number from Settings → About, and what happened.
