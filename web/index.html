Questions

Does loge come with anything to watch?

Catalogs, yes. Streams come from your addons; loge hosts nothing.

Which addons work?

Any addon with HTTP(S) streams. Torrent addons need a debrid service. Subtitle addons work too.

Why isn’t it in the App Store?

No paid developer account. Free Apple ID installs last seven days; SideStore and atvloadly renew them for you.

Can I download for offline viewing?

No, loge streams.

Something doesn’t work.

Open an issue with your device, build number (Settings → About) and what happened.
