About

loge is an app for watching what your Stremio addons find, built natively for each Apple device rather than wrapped in a web view. It opens on artwork, lets you pick exactly which stream to play and hands it to mpv.

The streams come from addons you install yourself. loge brings catalogs to browse but does not host, bundle or sell any content.

Platforms 4 from one SwiftUI app, each with its own layout

What it does

Browse

CinemetaTMDB

Popular films and series, Letterboxd lists, genres, festival winners and directors are built in, so Home is full before you add anything. Title pages bring seasons, Up Next, cast, reviews and More Like This.

Sources

Every addon

Each stream is read into resolution, HDR, codec, audio, size and bitrate, then grouped by quality. loge remembers the release you chose for the next episode and falls back to another source when a link fails.

Playback

mpv

Hardware-decoded MKV, HEVC and HLS. Subtitles from files and subtitle addons, audio in your language, intro and credits skips, Up Next, Picture in Picture and AirPlay.

Library

TraktSelf-hosted

Continue Watching, Watch Later, your own lists and a history by day. Progress follows you between devices through Trakt or a sync service you run yourself.

Apple TV

tvOS

A focus-driven Home that fills the screen with artwork, the Siri Remote throughout, and frame-rate and dynamic-range matching for playback.

Questions

Does loge come with anything to watch?

Catalogs to browse, not streams. Playback comes from the Stremio addons you install.

Which addons work?

Any addon that returns HTTP(S) streams. Torrent results are left out, so torrent addons need a debrid service that turns them into links. Subtitle addons work too.

Why isn’t it in the App Store?

It is a personal project without a paid developer account. With a free Apple ID, iPhone and Apple TV installs last seven days; SideStore and atvloadly refresh them.

Can I download for offline viewing?

No, loge streams.

Something doesn’t work.

Open an issue with your device, the build number from Settings → About and what happened.
